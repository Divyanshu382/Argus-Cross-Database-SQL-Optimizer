## Choosing exact or estimate (unseen datasets, deployed model)

*Agreement* is how often the policy made the same choice as an oracle that knows the real times and picks the estimate whenever it is really at least 1.5× faster (the same bar the model uses, since an estimate also carries error); *total time* is what running every query by that policy would have cost.

*Slowdown* is the geometric mean, per query, of the chosen time over the oracle's (1.00× is perfect); it weighs a millisecond query made 2× slower as much as a minute-long one.
